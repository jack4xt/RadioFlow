Privacy Policy – RadioFlow

Last updated: September 13, 2026

1. Introduction

RadioFlow is an application for playing internet radio stations. We respect the privacy of our users, and the application is designed so that users are not required to provide personal information.

2. Personal Information

RadioFlow does not require registration or the creation of a user account. The application itself does not collect, sell, or share users' personal information for advertising or analytical tracking purposes.

3. Internet Connection

RadioFlow uses an internet connection to load information about radio stations and to play their audio streams.

When connecting to internet radio streams, technical processing of the connection may be performed by the operator of the respective stream. Such processing is governed by the privacy policy of the relevant provider.

4. Data Stored on the Device

RadioFlow may store application settings and other information necessary for the proper functioning of the application, such as a list of favorite radio stations. This data is stored locally on the user's device and is not sent by the application to our server.

5. Device Permissions

The application may use Android system permissions required for audio playback in the background, displaying notifications, using alarms, and properly operating the application.

6. Advertising and Analytics

RadioFlow does not contain advertising services or third-party analytics tools used to track users.

7. Third-Party Services

RadioFlow plays internet radio streams provided by individual radio station operators. These operators may process technical information related to connections to their services. Each respective operator is responsible for how it handles such information.

8. Children

RadioFlow is not designed to collect personal information from children or other users.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary, for example when the features of the application change. The current version of this Privacy Policy will always be available on this page.

10. Contact

If you have any questions regarding privacy or the RadioFlow application, you can contact the developer through the contact information provided on the application's Google Play page.
